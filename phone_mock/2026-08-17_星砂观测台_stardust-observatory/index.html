Orion & Perseids Radiant
Aug 12, 2026 Tianzhu Mountain
Conditions
4.8
Visibility
21:00
Local Time
18°C
Temperature
Notes

Exceptional night at Tianzhu Mountain. The Perseids radiant was clearly visible in the northeast after midnight. Counted approximately 42 meteors in 3 hours of observation, including 3 bright fireballs leaving persistent trains.

Orion rose around 2 AM, providing a stunning backdrop with Betelgeuse glowing red and Rigel blazing blue-white. The seeing was steady at around 4.8/5 — rare for this altitude. Managed to capture the Orion Nebula with a 30-second exposure at ISO 3200.

Equipment

Camera: Sony A7IV
Lens: 24mm f/1.4 GM
Tripod: Gitzo GT3543LS
Tracker: iOptron SkyGuider Pro
